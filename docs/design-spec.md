# Valen — design spec

**Direction** — A black page, artwork-led colour, floating chrome. The language comes from two apps Valen already builds on: Mochy Music (full-bleed hero that fades into the page, tight semi-bold type, superellipse covers, a floating capsule player and tab bar, the cover's colours as drifting light) and Orpheon's Immersive look (black page with white-alpha text steps, translucent bars with a light edge, white chips, cover-flush quick tiles, a stage of bare transport glyphs over the cover's tint). Valen's own part is the second voice in the type and the way the same pieces serve a desktop window.

Not: stacked glass, decorative gradients unrelated to artwork, stock card grids, uppercase eyebrow labels, icons in circles as empty states, purple-blue gradients, emoji icons, placeholder copy, shadows.

**Type** — Two voices, one rule for which speaks.
- *The app* speaks in Bricolage Grotesque SemiBold: the greeting, screen titles, section titles, the wordmark. Scale: 60/60 (−0.035em) · 44/46 (−0.03em) · 34/36 (−0.03em) · 28/32 (−0.025em) · 22/26 (−0.02em) · 19/24 (−0.02em).
- *The music* is set in Inter: every song title, artist and control. Titles and labels are SemiBold, secondary lines Medium; nothing is Regular. Scale: 18/24 (−0.03em) · 15/20 (−0.025em) · 13/18 (−0.02em) semi-bold; 16/24 (−0.02em) · 14/20 (−0.015em) · 13/18 (−0.012em) medium; 11/14 medium for times (12/16 under the stage rail).
- Song titles as headlines (`TrackType`): 52/54 on a wide Home hero, 40/44 on the stage and the phone hero, 24/28 on the phone stage and beside the queue, all Inter SemiBold at −0.035 to −0.045em. Song titles are Inter, never Bricolage, because they arrive in any script and must not change typeface mid-title.
- Tracking tightens as size grows. **Nothing is uppercase.** Times and counts use tabular figures (`timeStyle`).
- A lead-in is two-tone, not a label above a title: "Playing from" at 62% white, then the service in full white.
- Assets: static TTFs bundled at `ui/src/commonMain/composeResources/font/` — `bricolage_semibold.ttf`, `inter_regular.ttf`, `inter_medium.ttf`, `inter_semibold.ttf`. Both families OFL. No system font is requested.

**Space & shape** — 4pt grid: 4 8 12 16 20 24 32 40 48 64 96. Gutters: phone 20, rail 28, desktop 40. Row 64.
Shapes are superellipses (`Squircle`, exponent 5), given as *reach* along the edge, which reads tighter than a circular radius of the same number: covers in rows 12 · rows, quick tiles 18 · grouped cards, shelf covers 28 · stage cover, hero cover, side panel 40. Controls a finger presses (buttons, chips, the search field, the dock, the tab bar) are pills. One shape per component. A cover that fills the end of a tile has square corners and is rounded by the tile, so the two share one curve.

**Colour (dark only)** — page `#000000` · surface `#141416` · surface2 `#1F1F23` · skeleton `#161618`. Text is white at three alphas, so the same steps work on the page, on a surface and on a wash: 100% · 62% · 38%. Ink on white controls `#000000`. Separator 1dp at 10% white, only between rows of one group. Fills for controls on glass or a wash: white at 10% (rest), 16% (strong/hover), 6% (hover over nothing). Scrim over a cover that carries a mark: black at 52%. Danger `#FF5A5F` · success `#3DD68C`.

**Artwork colour** — One accent per screen, from the artwork that is playing; fallback `#D9D9DE`. Pixels are grouped into 18 hue bins and weighted by saturation and brightness, so a gradient counts as the colour it reads as; a picture with under 6% coloured pixels gives its average grey. The winning hue is desaturated 15% and clamped to 30–55% luminance. The same measurement is taken for the left and right halves of the cover. Every use is a fixed luminance of those three colours (`ArtworkTones`), which is what keeps text legible on any cover:
- *Wash* (`AmbientWash`): a floor at 8% and three soft pools — left half at 30% (top left), right half at 26% (right), accent at 20% (low centre) — dithered, under a veil that fades toward the page. One wash per screen at most: behind the Home hero and greeting, and filling the stage, where it drifts slowly (96 s loop, 30 steps a second, draw phase only) while a song plays.
- *Player tint*: accent at 15%, mixed 62% into the glass of the dock and the mini player.
- *Marks on black*: accent at 74%, for the playing bars and the title of the row that is playing.
- Only covers of the playing song are measured (dock, mini player, hero, stage, the queue's now-playing cover).

**Chrome** — Floating, translucent, single layer: fill `#1E1E22` at 88% with a 1dp edge at 12% white. The side panel, the player dock, the phone's mini player and tab bar, and toasts are the only glass, and glass never sits on glass. Chrome is inset 12dp from the window and from other chrome. Content scrolls under the bottom chrome and dims to black beneath it.

**Motion** (one file: `Motion.kt`) — standard 280ms `cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)`; enter 320ms `cubic-bezier(.32,.72,0,1)`; exit 200ms `cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1)`; press 120ms, scale .97; page change 160ms cross-fade, never a slide; artwork crossfade 320ms; sheet 320/240ms; player tint 600ms; wash to a new cover 1400ms; selection pill spring(stiffness medium-low, damping .82); queue reorder spring(520, damping .83). A confirmation toast leaves after 2.6s; a provider's message stays until dismissed. Reduced motion: 120ms fades, 160ms tweens, no drift, no travel.

**Interaction** — One pressable (`Modifier.tap`): no ripple; hover and keyboard focus lift the fill to 6% white, press to 10%. Rows let that fill bleed 8dp past the text column so covers and titles stay on the page edge. Rails (seek, volume) thicken under a pointer or a finger and have no thumb. Small controls draw at 34–36dp but answer across 44dp.

**Components** — AppShell (floating `Sidebar` + `PlayerBar` dock / `BottomNav` + `MiniPlayer` capsules) · `PageTitle` · `SectionHeader` (title, chevron when it leads somewhere) · `SearchField` (52dp capsule) · `FilterChips` (white when on) · `SegmentedFilter` (sliding white pill) · `ResultRow` (playing state, queued tick) · `QueueRow` (drag handle) · `QuickTile` · `ShelfCard` · `ServiceTile` (tile on Home, row in a Settings group) · `Artwork` (crossfade, never shimmer) · `AmbientWash` · `TransportBar` (bare glyphs) · `SeekBar` (6→10dp, elapsed and time left) · `InlineSeek` (dock) · `VolumeSlider` · `NoticeToast` · `InlineError` · `EmptyState` · `SkeletonRows` (flat blocks shaped like the rows to come).

**Screens**
- *Home* — What is playing as the top of the page: on a wide window a wash with the words bottom-left and the cover right; on a phone the cover itself, edge to edge, fading into the page. Then Up next (quick tiles: 3 / 2 / 1 across), Recently played (a shelf of covers), Services. With nothing played: greeting, one sentence, a door to search. A section with nothing to show is not shown.
- *Search* — Title, field, chips ("All", or the services picked). Before a query: what one search does and the order services play in, with the way to change it. Results are one list; a failed service is named above it.
- *Now Playing* — The wash fills the screen. Wide and landscape: cover left, the song beside it. Otherwise: cover on top. Words set left. Seek, bare transport (play glyph 68–72dp), volume where there is room, then what comes next as the way into the queue.
- *Queue* — Title and "24 songs · 1 hr 42 min". With room (≥920dp) the playing song stands beside the list as a cover; without, it leads the list. Up next (draggable), then Played (dimmed).
- *Settings* — A 720dp column. Each group is a title, one line saying what it does, and one raised surface with separators: Services, Sources, Audio quality (segmented), About.

**Layout** — Screens draw edge to edge; `LocalChrome` tells them what the chrome takes, and `ContentColumn` keeps content clear of it and inside the gutters at the full width the window gives — a wide window is room for more music, not more margin. Only a screen that is read rather than browsed passes `maxWidth` (Settings: a 720dp reading column, centred). Grids earn a column for every ~300–350dp (Up next: 1–6 across), and the two big artworks grow with the window (hero cover 248/312/360, stage cover up to 720) so they are never a stamp in the corner of a wide band. ≥1000dp: 236dp floating side panel + 72dp floating dock (max 1000dp, centred under the content; gives up volume, then times, then the rail as it narrows). 700–999: the panel becomes a 68dp icon rail. <700: 60dp tab bar capsule (Home, Search, Now Playing; the current one spells its name) + 64dp mini player capsule; Now Playing is a full-screen sheet, the queue a second sheet. Navigation state lives above the width switch, so resizing across a breakpoint keeps your place. Safe areas respected; desktop min 900×640; phone reference 390×844.

**Rules** — One accent and at most one wash per screen. Hit targets ≥44dp. Rows truncate at one line, headlines at two. Material Symbols Rounded only; no service logos. Empty states are a title and a sentence about real state on the page's left edge, with at most one action (the stage alone centres its text). Provider failures are shown in the provider's words and never time out.
